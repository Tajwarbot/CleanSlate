## Design Context

### Users
Privacy-conscious Facebook and Messenger users who want to clean their own activity safely from a browser extension.

### Brand Personality
Transparent, technical, and controlled. The interface should explain what will happen before it happens and never hide a destructive step.

### Aesthetic Direction
Brutalist utility: direct labels, visible process states, strong contrast, compact structure, and no decorative ambiguity. Support both light and dark themes with clear focus states and readable contrast.

### Design Principles
1. Make the next action and its consequence explicit.
2. Separate platform-specific workflows so Facebook controls never appear in Messenger flows.
3. Verify the user is on the correct destination before scanning or changing anything.
4. Show progress as a concrete sequence: choose, verify, load, review, confirm, execute.
5. Preserve keyboard access, readable contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.
